# TUNKOW — Lovable.dev Master Prompt

> Copy and paste this prompt directly into **Lovable.dev** (or **v0.dev** / **Bolt.new**) to generate the official website for Tunkow.

```markdown
Build a world-class, responsive official website for the Latin Ska band **TUNKOW** (Berlin-based Latin Ska ensemble, formerly Bodgo Skatz).

### 🏛️ Brand & Aesthetic (Andean Modernist)
- **Concept:** Andean Modernism — architectural, minimalist, museum-grade aesthetic inspired by pre-Columbian Andean monoliths and Swiss International Style, combined with the raw live energy of the Berlin underground.
- **The Tunqui Connection:** Named after the *Tunqui* (Andean Cock-of-the-rock), Peru's scarlet national bird known for its hypnotic communal dance rituals (*leks*).
- **Color System:**
  - Background: Crisp Gallery Chalk White (`#FAFAFA`) as default, with option to toggle Dark Stage Mode (`#0C0D0F`).
  - Primary Accent: Surgical Tunqui Cinnabar Scarlet (`#D81616`) for buttons, active tabs, and highlights.
  - Typography Color: Deep Basalt Charcoal (`#101114`) on light mode; Chalk White (`#FAFAFA`) on dark mode.
  - Metadata & Muted: Andean Mist Grey (`#9A9FA8`).
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Hero: 'Unbounded' (Google Font), 800 ExtraBold with wide letter-spacing.
  - Subheadings & Navigation: 'Space Grotesk' (Google Font), 700 Bold uppercase.
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', clean, readable, humanist.

---

### 📱 Key Sections & Interactive Features

1. **Sticky Header & Navigation:**
   - Brand Logo: The TUNKOW wordmark with Tunqui crest icon.
   - Nav Links: Music, Tour Dates, The Ensemble, Merch, EPK / Booking.
   - Right Actions: Language Switcher (EN | ES | DE), Light/Dark Mode toggle (Gallery White vs. Dark Stage), and a bold red "TICKETS" button.

2. **Hero Section (Luminous & High-Impact):**
   - Airy gallery white layout with high-contrast, editorial framing of the band performing live under warm concert lights.
   - Massive headline: "T U N K O W" with the tagline: "EL LEK URBANO &bull; BERLIN".
   - Primary Call to Action: "GET TOUR TICKETS" + "LISTEN TO NEW SINGLE".
   - Live Audio Player Bar: Embedded preview bar with play/pause, scrubbable progress bar, volume control, and animated audio equalizer wave for the track *"El Salto del Tunqui"*.

3. **The Musician Ensemble Showcase:**
   - Interactive grid/carousel highlighting the band members and instrumentation:
     - Horn Section: Trumpet, Saxophone, Trombone
     - Rhythm Section: Drums, Congas/Percussion, Bass
     - Front & Melody: Lead Harmonica & Vocals, Electric Guitar, Keys & Synth, Strings
   - Hover cards showing member portraits, instrument icons, and brief playful quotes.

4. **Live Tour & Festival Calendar:**
   - Upcoming gig listings (e.g. SO36 Berlin, Gebäude 9 Köln, Hafenklang Hamburg, Parc del Fòrum Barcelona).
   - Each row displays: Date badge, City, Venue name, Status (On Sale / Low Tickets / Sold Out), and a direct "Buy Tickets" button.
   - Filter by: "Upcoming 2024", "Festival Dates", "Past Shows".

5. **Music & Discography:**
   - 12" Vinyl Album Showcase: 3D interactive vinyl sleeve for *"El Lek Urbano"* with tracklist (Side A & Side B).
   - Direct streaming integration buttons: Spotify, Apple Music, Bandcamp, YouTube Music.
   - "Order Vinyl LP" modal with format specs (180g heavyweight vinyl, gatefold sleeve).

6. **Merchandise Storefront Preview:**
   - Product cards:
     - "Tour 2024 Tee (Washed Basalt Black)"
     - "Organic Canvas Bone Tote Bag"
     - "Polished Gunmetal Tunqui Hard Enamel Pin"
     - "Die-Cut Waterproof Sticker Pack"
   - Quick view modal with size selector (S, M, L, XL, XXL) and "Add to Bag" cart drawer.

7. **Electronic Press Kit (EPK) & Promoter Booking:**
   - Band Bio & Origin Story (from Peru to Berlin).
   - High-resolution press photo downloads (ZIP).
   - Stage Tech Rider & Channel Input List preview (essential for festival sound engineers).
   - Booking inquiry contact form (Name, Organization/Venue, Date, Message).

8. **Footer:**
   - Tunqui Disc Crest vector watermark, social links (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Spotify), newsletter signup ("Join the Lek"), and copyright notice.
```
