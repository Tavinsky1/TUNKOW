# TUNKOW — Adobe Firefly Master Prompt & Texture Guide
> Designed for **Adobe Firefly Image 3** (`https://firefly.adobe.com`) to generate non-vector, tactile, physical, and artisanal editions of the **TUNKOW** logo and Tunqui crest.

---

## 🛠️ Recommended Adobe Firefly Settings

To avoid the "flat vector / digital clipart" look, configure the Firefly sidebar with these exact settings:

| Setting | Recommended Value | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Model** | **Firefly Image 3** | Latest generative architecture with superior material textures. |
| **Content Type** | **Art** (or **Photo**) | ⚠️ **Never select "Graphic"** — Graphic forces flat vector Bézier lines. "Art" or "Photo" forces physical media and lighting. |
| **Aspect Ratio** | **Square (1:1)** | Best for logo badges, crest medallions, and vinyl center labels. |
| **Visual Intensity** | **4 / 5 or 5 / 5** | Maximizes paper fiber, chisel marks, ink bleed, and micro-texture. |
| **Structure Reference** | Upload `assets/tunkow-crest-artisan.svg` or `tunqui_icon.jpg` (Strength: 65%–80%) | Locks in the exact silhouette of the Tunqui bird and disc crest while applying new physical materials. |
| **Effects (in Firefly)** | *Woodblock print*, *Linocut*, *Etching*, or *Clay/Metal* | Adds organic artisanal grain. |

## 🎨 5 Battle-Tested Prompts for Adobe Firefly

### 1. 🪵 Hand-Carved Linocut & Woodblock Print (Xilografía)
> **Best for:** Album inner sleeves, tour posters, art prints, limited cassette inserts.

```text
Macro photograph of an authentic hand-carved woodblock relief print of the Peruvian Tunqui bird (Cock-of-the-rock) circular emblem with Andean geometric crest. Heavy archival vermilion red oil ink and matte carbon black ink hand-pressed onto thick, rough fibrous cotton rag paper. Visible gouge marks, wood grain ridges, chiseled knife facets, natural ink bleed at edges, deckled paper edge. Non-vector, organic printmaking craft, tactile analog fine art print, warm directional natural light.
```

* **Firefly Tags / Effects to check:** *Woodblock*, *Printmaking*, *Warm tone*, *Craft*.

### 2. 🗿 Andean Basalt Stone Relief (Tiwanaku / Chavín Monolith)
> **Best for:** Album cover monolith, stage backdrop projections, museum-grade merch.

```text

Inlaid with radiant raw cinnabar red mineral pigment inside the carved geometric incisions. Architectural stepped frets, rough porous stone micro-texture, chipped stone edges, dramatic raking museum light casting deep natural shadows. Sculptural, physical, non-vector artifact.

* **Firefly Tags / Effects to check:** *Chiseled*, *Stone*, *Dramatic light*, *High contrast*.
